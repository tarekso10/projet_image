\subsection{Conclusion and future work}
\label{sec:phase2}

Phase 1 established a processed dataset, metadata, normalized CLIP embeddings, train-only FAISS indexes, and basic retrieval checks. Phase 2 remains future work: generate captions, compare retrieval strategies under shared settings, evaluate caption quality and hallucinations, and conduct paired statistical tests and ablations. No caption-generation results or caption metrics are reported here.
